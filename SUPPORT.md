# KalkulaPH Support

Use this page for KalkulaPH calculation feedback, privacy questions, rate corrections and general support.

## How to get support

Open a new issue in this repository and describe the problem or question. Please include the calculator or screen involved, the expected result, and the result you received when relevant.

**Do not post government ID numbers, account credentials, medical information, bank details or other sensitive personal information in a public issue.**

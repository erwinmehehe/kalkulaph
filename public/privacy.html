KalkulaPH Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

KalkulaPH is an independent calculator app developed by Erwin Valles for Philippine salary, tax, contribution, benefits and personal-planning estimates.

Information stored on your device

KalkulaPH stores job-history entries that you choose to save, cached verified statutory-rate data, and the timestamp of the most recent rate check locally on your device. Calculator inputs are used to produce estimates and are not uploaded to a KalkulaPH account or server.

KalkulaPH does not require an account, name, email address, phone number, government ID, bank connection, contacts, photos, location or advertising identifier to use its calculators.

Data collection and sharing

The current app does not include advertising, analytics, cloud sync, crash-reporting or user-account SDKs. KalkulaPH does not automatically transmit your salary inputs, calculator values or saved job history to the developer or sell or share them with third parties.

Automatic rate checks

When internet access is available, KalkulaPH may request a small public JSON file hosted on GitHub to check the verified SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG and tax parameters supported by the app. Salary values, calculator inputs and job-history records are not included in that request. Standard network metadata such as IP address and request headers may be processed by GitHub as part of serving the file.

Other network use

Core calculations can run offline. Internet access may also be used when you choose to open a public support or privacy page or an official source operated by agencies such as SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG Fund, BIR, DOLE or NWPC. Those services have their own privacy practices.

Retention and deletion

Local app data remains on your device until you remove it. You can delete KalkulaPH local data through Settings → Clear all local data, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app.

Security

KalkulaPH minimizes data collection by keeping user-entered financial and employment information on-device. Public rate checks and web links use HTTPS.

Children

KalkulaPH is a general finance and salary utility and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Independent service

KalkulaPH is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or an official app of SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG Fund, BIR, DOLE or NWPC. Calculator outputs are educational estimates and may differ from final agency or employer calculations.

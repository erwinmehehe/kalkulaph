KalkulaPH Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

KalkulaPH is a calculator-first salary companion developed by Erwin Valles for Philippine salary, tax, contribution, worker-benefit and personal planning estimates.

Information stored on your device

KalkulaPH may store saved calculation results and their inputs, an optional My Salary profile, job-history entries you choose to save, payday and reminder preferences, favorite and recently used calculators, cached validated contribution-rate data, and display preferences locally on your device.

KalkulaPH does not require your name, email address, phone number, employer account, government ID or bank connection to use its core calculators or salary companion tools.

Data collection and sharing

The current production design does not include advertising, analytics, cloud-sync, crash-reporting or user-account SDKs. KalkulaPH does not automatically transmit your calculator inputs, saved calculations, My Salary profile or job history to the developer.

Automatic contribution-rate check

When internet access is available, KalkulaPH may automatically request a small public JSON contribution-rate manifest from the KalkulaPH GitHub Pages site. The request is used to check supported SSS, PhilHealth and Pag-IBIG contribution parameters. KalkulaPH does not include salary values, calculator inputs, saved results or job-history content in that request. Standard network metadata may be processed by the hosting provider as part of serving the file.

Other network use

Core calculations can run offline. Internet access is also used when you choose to open an official-government source link, the public privacy/support pages, or the public rate manifest.

Sharing

If you explicitly choose to share a result card or report an issue, your device's native sharing interface opens and you choose the destination. KalkulaPH does not automatically send the shared content anywhere.

External services and links

KalkulaPH links to official resources operated by agencies such as SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG Fund, BIR, DOLE and NWPC and uses GitHub Pages for its public privacy/support pages and contribution-rate manifest. Those services have their own privacy practices.

Retention and deletion

Local app data remains on your device until you delete it. You can remove local data through More → Reset local data, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app.

Children

KalkulaPH is a general salary and benefits utility and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Independent service

KalkulaPH is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or an official app of SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG Fund, BIR, DOLE or NWPC. Calculator outputs are estimates.
